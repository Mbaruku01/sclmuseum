Artifact of the Week

Sculpting History: Dinosaurs from Metal Waste

Triassic Period
National Museum of Tanzania

Metal wastes were carefully collected and repurposed to create a welded sculpture in the shape of a dinosaur. This approach transforms discarded materials into a striking piece of art, serving as a tangible preservation of history—connecting viewers with the prehistoric past in a modern, sustainable form.
