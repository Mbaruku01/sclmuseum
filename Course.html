Why Study at the Faculty of Arts and Social Sciences?

The Faculty of Arts and Social Sciences (FASS) comprises 6 specialized departments offering a comprehensive academic ladder—from Certificate and Diploma levels to Bachelor, Postgraduate, and PhD research.

Established in 1992, FASS is the Open University of Tanzania’s oldest faculty. We are pioneers in Distance Learning across East Africa, utilizing advanced ICT, telecasting, and enhanced face-to-face seminars.
